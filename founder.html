Biography

Leadership with a compliance standard

Randy Baker founded Mission Ready Medical Staffing to bring a clear standard to local CNA and RN coverage: do not send a clinician until the file is ready.

He brings 35 years of high-stakes compliance experience to healthcare staffing. That background shapes how the company reviews credentials, documents files, and treats every open shift as a responsibility to patients and facilities.

As a 100% disabled veteran, he holds the team to accountability, preparation, and follow-through. The result is a staffing partner built around zero-defect compliance, 24/7 responsiveness, and reliable shift coverage in Southern California and San Diego County.
