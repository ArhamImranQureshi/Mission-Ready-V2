Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

Mission Ready Medical Staffing collects only the information you choose to send through our facility request form, clinician application, or email.

What we collect

Facility requests may include contact details, facility name, staffing needs, and related notes. Clinician applications may include contact details, license information, availability, optional notes, and an optional resume file.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to staffing requests and applications. We do not sell personal information.

Storage

If a submission service is configured, data is processed by that provider and delivered to our team. Resume files are not stored in a public website folder. Until a submission service is connected, please email info@missionreadymedicalstaffing.com instead of relying on the web form for delivery.

Contact

Privacy questions: info@missionreadymedicalstaffing.com.
