How it works

A simple application process

We verify licenses and credentials before any shift is offered. Scheduling is flexible and subject to available shifts.

  1. STEP 1

    Apply

    Submit your details and, if you have one, a resume in PDF or Word format.

  2. STEP 2

    Verify

    We confirm your CNA or RN license and review credentials at the source.

  3. STEP 3

    Clear

    Your file completes our compliance-first, audit-ready vetting process.

  4. STEP 4

    Work

    When a matching local shift is open, we contact you with the assignment details.

Expectations

What we look for

01

Active license

A current California CNA or RN license, or another U.S. license if you are eligible to work locally.

02

Complete credentials

Be ready to provide certifications, identification, and other documents needed for facility clearance.

03

Professional reliability

Show up prepared, communicate early about schedule changes, and meet the facility’s standards of care.
